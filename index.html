About

I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Southern Denmark, where I work in the Historical Economics and Development Group (HEDG). I am supervised by Karol Jan Borowiecki and Julius Koschnick.

My research is in economic history and cultural economics. I study how markets for creative goods develop over the long run, and how institutions, technology and politics shape competition, specialization and the careers of creators. This work relates to research on copyright and creativity, on the historical organization of cultural industries, and on the geography of creative production.

Methodologically, I combine large digitized historical sources with econometric methods.

Fields: Economic History · Cultural Economics · Industrial Organization · Applied Econometrics
